# Projects (newest to oldest)

- **[stock_analysis](https://github.com/Jujordan/stock_analysis)**  
  Immediately after my 2026 summer course I started a project attempting to use AI tools to develop a library for R Studio to analyze stock tickers. The idea was to test the capabilities of AI Agents on the xAI platform, familiarize myself with prompting agents and implement sound programming design to create an R Studio library I could expand on and use. The substance of the project was a personal interest in stock analysis and performance. The main idea was take the performance of stock A and overlay stocks XYZ on it, taking any period of time and duration from stock A and overlaying any period of time and that same length of duration from XYZs.

- **[salary-project](https://github.com/Jujordan/salary-project)**  
  My MA3740 final project. A personal interest into financial salary data and public institutions and transparency of information. R Studio, AI prompts, and statistics principles above and beyond the course work were utilized with professor assistance.
